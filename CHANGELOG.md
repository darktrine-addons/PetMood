# Changelog

## Unreleased

### New features
- Movable pet happiness icon, with the classic Happy / Content / Unhappy faces.
- LibDataBroker feed with the coloured mood and icon.
- Mood drop alert: centre-screen warning, chat line and sound when your pet's mood drops. Drops during combat are announced when combat ends.
- Click to feed: click the locked icon (or use its key binding) to feed your pet the best-suited food from your bags.
- Tooltip shows loyalty, diet and the amount of food you carry.
- Slash commands: `/petmood lock`, `alert`, `sound`, `test`, `reset`, `diag`.

### Improvements
- Tooltips don't open in combat.
