# PetMood

**Keep your hunter pet happy without opening the pet window.**

PetMood shows your pet's happiness as a small icon you can move anywhere on screen. It warns you when the mood drops and lets you feed your pet with one click. Made for **WoW Forever**, where pet happiness is back.

## Features

- **Mood at a glance.** A small icon with the classic Happy / Content / Unhappy faces. Drag it wherever you like, then lock it in place.
- **Alert when the mood drops.** A centre-screen warning with a sound when your pet goes from Happy to Content or from Content to Unhappy. During a fight it waits until combat is over.
- **One-click feeding.** Click the icon, or press a key you bind, to feed your pet. PetMood picks the food itself: something your pet eats and that is close enough to its level, keeping your better food for later.
- **Everything in the tooltip.** Mood, damage bonus, loyalty, your pet's diet and how much food you carry.
- **Broker support.** Also shows the mood in Bazooka, Titan Panel, ElvUI and other LibDataBroker displays.
- **Out of your way.** Optionally hide the icon while your pet is Happy. No tooltips in combat. Inactive on characters that aren't hunters, so you can leave it enabled for all of them.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/petmood lock` | Lock the icon (click to feed) or unlock it (drag to move) |
| `/petmood happy` | Hide the icon while your pet is Happy |
| `/petmood alert` | Turn the mood drop alert on or off |
| `/petmood sound` | Turn the alert sound on or off |
| `/petmood test` | Show a test alert |
| `/petmood reset` | Move the icon back to the centre of the screen |

To feed with a key, bind one under **Key Bindings → AddOns → PetMood**.
